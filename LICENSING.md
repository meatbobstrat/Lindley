# Licensing Lindley

Lindley is free for a person to use for themselves. Organisations, and anyone using it to make
money, need a licence. The full terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE). This page says in plain words
what they mean.

## Free: your own use

If you're using Lindley for yourself, it's free. You don't need to ask. For example:

- tracing your family's history
- scanning your grandmother's letters, or your own papers
- a private collection, a hobby, or your own study and learning

You can also change Lindley and share your changes, as long as they carry the same licence.

## A licence: everyone else

You need a licence if you use Lindley for, or on behalf of, an organisation, or to make money.
For example:

- a professional genealogist doing work for clients
- a business of any size
- a research project at a university, or one paid for by a grant
- a museum, archive, library or historical society
- a school, charity or other non-profit
- a government office

## Trying it first

Anyone may use Lindley for up to 30 days to see whether it suits them, before buying a
licence.

## Licences

| Who | Price |
|---|---|
| Non-profits, charities, museums and schools | Reduced |
| Businesses | Standard |
| Government | Quoted on request, with a security review and support |

To ask about a licence, write to [me@bobstratton.net](mailto:me@bobstratton.net).

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Because Lindley is also licensed to organisations, each pull request
needs this line in its description:

> I agree that Bob Stratton may license my contribution under Lindley's licence, and under
> other licences, including commercial ones.
