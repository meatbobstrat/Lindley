# Changes

## 0.1.0 (in development)

The first version, for the MVP: scans dropped in a folder are read, sorted into documents and
made into searchable PDFs, and you can ask about them. A person's original scans are never
changed or deleted: a finished document is marked complete, then exported to the folder they
chose, with its original scans beside its PDF when Lindley moved them in. Installed from a Windows MSI, an Ubuntu
26.04 .deb, or a Mac app (unsigned).
